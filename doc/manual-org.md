#### GAME INSTRUCTIONS
The year is 2312. Due to nuclear contamination on the surface, the human colony on Zarkos has relocated entirely underground, deep within the planet's vast cave systems. However, these caverns are far from deserted; having previously served as a robotic testing ground, they are still overrun with rogue wandering machines. Because of the rugged subterranean terrain and low gravity, a spacecraft serves as the primary mode of transit.

You pilot this craft during the game. Thrust is generated by an underside-mounted rocket engine. By rotating clockwise or counterclockwise, you adjust the angle of thrust to maneuver in any direction. Gravity and the craft's inertia will also influence flight handling.

**Controls:**  
* **Rotate craft right:** Joystick right
* **Rotate craft left:** Joystick left
* **Thrust:** Press fire button
* **Fire:** Joystick up

#### The Enemies

* **Blobs:**  
Large floating entities that travel along predetermined routes. Colliding with a Blob or its projectiles is fatal. Blobs vary in:
    * Appearance and animation
    * Hits required to destroy
    * Firing frequency
    * Projectile speed
    * Projectile exit points
    * Firing range

* **Tanks:**  
Ground vehicles that drive across flat surfaces and fire intermittently. Most can be eliminated with a single hit.

* **Cannons:**  
Ground-mounted automated turrets that open fire whenever your ship enters their line of sight. Cannons vary in:
    * Hits required to destroy
    * Number of simultaneous shots
    * Projectile formation
    * Firing frequency
    * Projectile speed
    * Firing range

#### Game Modes
* **Mission:** Your objective is to transport cargo from the caves back to base. Your ship can carry either up to three small containers or one large and one small container. Carrying more cargo increases ship weight, making flight handling noticeably heavier. Once all containers have been delivered to base or lost to accidents, the mission is complete and the next stage loads. There are 49 caves in total; every 5th level can be accessed directly from the start using a password received upon first reaching it. The game ends when all ships are destroyed by collisions or enemy fire. The top ten high scores and player names are saved to disk.

* **Race:** Compete in the popular Zarkos cave races. Select one of 10 tracks from the main menu using the joystick (left or right). Each course is marked by eight numbered gates. Flying through Gate 1 starts the timer. Races run for three laps, with Gate 8 acting as the finish line. All three enemy types can spawn in the caverns. Crashing immediately ends the race; setting a new track record will save your time and name to disk.

* **2 Pilot Race:** Two players race head-to-head on split-screen. Choose from 3 courses in the main menu using the joystick (left or right). Tracks use the eight-gate layout, but no lap times are recorded. The sole objective is crossing the finish line before your opponent. Crashed ships instantly respawn at the starting gate with unlimited lives.

* **Dogfight:** Two players engage in direct aerial combat. Choose between 2 arenas in the main menu using the joystick (left or right). The first pilot to lose all ships loses the duel. Any of the six levels can be selected freely from the main menu.
