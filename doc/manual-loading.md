### GRAVITY FORCE
**INSTRUCTIONS**
---

#### LOADING
* **Atari ST:** Insert the game disk into the disk drive and press the reset button on the computer. The program will load and launch automatically. The disk must not be write-protected and must remain in the drive until the computer is reset!

* **Amiga:** Turn on your computer and insert the KICKSTART disk (Version 1.2 or 1.3) into the built-in drive (Amiga 1000 only). When prompted for the WORKBENCH disk (V1.2/V1.3), insert the game disk. The game will load and launch automatically. The disk must not be write-protected and must remain in the drive until the computer is reset.

* **IBM PC:** Turn on your computer and boot DOS. Insert the game disk into drive A: and type: **K S** (RETURN). The program will load and launch automatically. The disk must not be write-protected and must remain in the drive until the computer is reset!

* **C-64/128 Cassette:** If using a C-128, switch to C-64 mode first. Insert the game cassette into the tape deck and rewind if necessary. Press the **SHIFT** and **RUN/STOP** keys simultaneously on the computer. The screen will display: "PRESS PLAY ON TAPE". Press the **PLAY** button on the tape deck. The program will load and launch automatically.

* **C-64/128 Disk:** If using a C-128, switch to C-64 mode first. Insert the game disk into the drive and enter: **LOAD "*", 8, 2** (RETURN). The program will load and launch automatically.

* **Plus/4 Cassette:** Insert the game cassette into the tape deck and rewind if necessary. Type: **LOAD** (RETURN). The screen will display: "PRESS PLAY ON TAPE". Press the **PLAY** button on the tape deck. The program will load and launch automatically.

* **Plus/4 Disk:** Insert the game disk into the drive and press the **SHIFT** and **RUN/STOP** keys simultaneously on the computer. The program will load and launch automatically.
